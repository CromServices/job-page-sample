What stands out

  1. High

    The first screen is slow to finish on mobile

    The largest thing on the first screen is what a shopper waits to see; when it arrives late, people leave before they can buy.

  2. High

    The first-screen slideshow image is heavy and late

    The first-screen image often is the largest content; a heavy or late-loading slide delays the moment the shopper can read the offer.

  3. High

    The page stays busy before it can respond to taps

    When the main thread is busy, the page looks as if it has loaded but does not yet respond to taps.

  4. Medium

    Useful content appears after one second on this lab run

    Until the first useful content appears, the shopper is looking at a blank or unfinished screen.

  5. Low

    Several apps and tags load with the page

    Extra apps and tags each add requests and script work on every page that uses the shared templates.
