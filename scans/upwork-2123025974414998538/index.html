  2. Medium

    The meta description is outside the usual length

    The meta description is often the snippet under the title in search results, and helps people decide whether to click.

  3. Medium

    The homepage title is outside the usual length

    The title is the main line search engines and tabs show; a missing or awkward title makes the page harder to find and click.

  4. Medium

    robots.txt has no sitemap line

    robots.txt is the first file crawlers read; if it is missing or blocks the homepage, the site may not be found.

    Most images on this page are missing alt text

    Alt text describes images when they do not load and helps the page show up in image search.
