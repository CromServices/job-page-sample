What stands out

    Exact-keyword slugs return 404

    https://www.groupescapehouses.co.uk/large-houses-for-groups and https://www.groupescapehouses.co.uk/group-holiday-houses both return 404. This finding is from a follow-up URL probe; it is not part of the 92 / 100 homepage grade above.

  3. Medium

    Several live pages target overlapping head terms

    The intended page at /large-group-accommodation is live (200), with title “Large Group Accommodation UK | Large Houses for Groups”. The homepage title and H1 still speak to “Group Houses Across the UK”, and /properties is titled “Large Houses to Rent in the UK | Browse All Group Houses”. This overlap can keep rankings from settling on one URL. This finding is from a follow-up URL probe; it is not part of the 92 / 100 homepage grade above.

  4. Medium

    The meta description is outside the usual length

    The meta description is often the snippet under the title in search results, and helps people decide whether to click.

  5. Low

    The page is slow or very heavy

    Slow or heavy pages lose visitors and are harder for search engines to rate well.
