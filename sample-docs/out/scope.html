A fixed-price build of a simple order-ahead page for a small fictional café. All names and figures are made up for this sample.

02Goal

Let regulars order coffee and breakfast ahead from their phone, so the morning queue moves faster.

03In scope

  • One mobile-first ordering page with the café menu
  • Order-ahead form with pickup time
  • Order emails to the café inbox
  • A short handover guide

04Out of scope

  • Online payments
  • Delivery or driver tracking
  • New logo, photography or copywriting
  • Changes to the café's other pages

05Deliverables

  • Ordering page, live on the café's site
  • Menu set-up from the café's current menu
  • Handover guide for updating prices and items
