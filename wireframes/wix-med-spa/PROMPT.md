# Bid wireframe: existing Wix med spa redesign

Quick bid wireframe, 16 Nov 2026, for Director. Built from the portfolio (only citable Wix row: **general-power-wix**, https://www.generalpower.com.au — a trades rebuild, not a med spa; do not present it as one). No brand file or site URL was given; the nav uses a simple mark labelled "Client brand". Service names are labelled "Sample content". Not a full build. Do not publish.

## The build

"I need a freelancer to redesign my existing med spa website to create a more polished, professional, and visually appealing experience."

"The work includes updating the homepage, services page, and contact page, improving overall layout and navigation, and refining the site’s look and feel to better reflect my brand."

"I’m looking for someone who can deliver a clean, modern redesign that is easy to use and helps improve the overall customer experience."

## Feeling from the scope

Visitors should feel they have arrived "somewhere polished, calm, and professionally cared-for." Arriving, "at ease and quietly impressed; the space should feel clean, modern, and true to the spa's own brand." Exploring, "clear and comfortable, able to see services without being crowded or sold to." Deciding, "ready to get in touch, with a simple path that still feels considered." After, "looked after."

## Concept

A quieter med spa. Warm stone, a muted rose-brown, and no promo shouting. The hero is a treatment in soft light. Home, services and contact share one typography and one path. Treatments sit in an unhurried grid, labelled as sample content until their own names are in place. Getting in touch is a short, considered form.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: "a more polished, professional, and visually appealing experience," "clean, modern," "easy to use," and "somewhere polished, calm, and professionally cared-for," "at ease and quietly impressed," able to see services "without being crowded or sold to."

**At ease in a space that is polished, calm, and professionally cared-for.**

Not a task. The quiet of a well-kept room.

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — ease
- Feeling word: ease
- Inner voice: “Clean light, unhurried space. This feels like their rooms.”
- Design levers: a warm treatment photograph flush under a pale nav; a Client brand mark on a soft plate; a two-to-three-line H1; a dusty-rose rule; wide space and few words.

#### Explore — comfort
- Feeling word: comfort
- Inner voice: “The treatments are in front of me, with room to breathe.”
- Design levers: three calm service cards; sample names clearly labelled; a quieter look-and-feel passage; restrained type and spacing.

#### Decide — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “Getting in touch looks as considered as the rooms themselves.”
- Design levers: one obvious contact path from the hero and from services; no stacked promo offers.

#### Act — considered
- Feeling word: considered
- Inner voice: “A short message will do. Nothing is crowding the page.”
- Design levers: a short contact form; 50px fields; a full-width button that does not clip; the same calm type as the rest of the site.

#### After — cared
- Feeling word: cared
- Inner voice: “Coming back later, the path is still easy to find.”
- Design levers: a quiet thank-you; no upsell; footer repeating Home, Services, Contact.

Never feel:

- Cluttered or dated
- Harsh-clinical
- Cheap promo, or overwhelmed
- Sold to before they are ready
- Lost between home, services and contact
