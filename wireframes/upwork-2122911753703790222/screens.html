Hand-off: edit a page, publish, add a blog post

  1. Open any page in Wix Studio (Home, About, Services, Journal or Contact). Change the words or pictures, then press Publish. The live site updates. No code.
  2. To add a journal post, open Blog and choose New post. Fill the fields on this screen: title, tag, date, excerpt, body and cover.
  3. Press Publish to the journal. The post template uses those fields, and the “latest from the journal” slot on Home updates from the same collection.
