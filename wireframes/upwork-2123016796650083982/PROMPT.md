# Bid wireframe: Parramatta plumber WordPress rebuild (Upwork 2123016796650083982)

Quick pre-bid wireframe, 18 Nov 2026, for Director. No business name, logo, licence or reviews were supplied. Your Plumbing Co is a clearly labelled sample name. All copy is marked Sample content. Dry-run publish only.

## The build

Quoted from the client’s Upwork post:

- "rebuild our current website into a modern, fast, mobile-friendly site that we can easily update ourselves."
- "Around 60 suburb pages (e.g. /plumber-parramatta) plus service pages"
- "protecting our rankings is very important"
- "Mobile-first design with a tap-to-call button on every page"
- "A shorter, cleaner homepage (around 1,000 words) focused on Parramatta and Western Sydney"
- "All existing service and suburb pages moved across"
- "301 redirects from EVERY old page address to the new pages"
- "Proper page titles and meta descriptions, Google Search Console connected, fast loading speed"
- "Google reviews, licence details and a simple quote form on the site"
- "A short video or session showing us how to edit text and photos"
- "Domain, hosting and all logins set up in OUR name"

## Coverage checklist

| Requirement | Location |
|-------------|------------------|
| Mobile-first, tap-to-call on every page | Sticky call bar on every public phone frame; header “Tap to call” on desktop (`#home`, `#suburb`, `#service`, `#areas`, `#quote`) |
| Short homepage (~1,000 words) focused on Parramatta and Western Sydney | `#home`: hero, services, areas, how it works, reviews band, licence strip, quote CTA |
| Service pages moved across | `#service` blocked drains page (with hot water and taps also linked) |
| ~60 suburb pages moved across, same URLs | `#suburb` at `/plumber-parramatta`, plus `#areas` index listing all 60 suburb URLs |
| Simple quote form | `#quote`: job type, suburb, urgency, photo upload, preferred time |
| Google reviews and licence details | `#home-reviews` band (“Google reviews appear here — connected at build”) and `#home-licence` (“[Licence no. — added by client]”) |
| 301 redirects from every old address; rankings protected | `#admin-inbox` redirect map table (old URL, new URL, 301, checked) with 60 of 60 coverage meter |
| Titles, meta, Search Console, speed | `#admin-seo`: SEO per page (title, meta, indexed) and Search Console connected tile |
| Easy self-editing (Elementor-style) | `#admin-edit`: owner edits text and swaps a photo, plus “how to edit” video tile |
| Domain, hosting, logins in their name | `#admin-seo` handover checklist |
| FOCUS (Nate): admin and booking side | `#admin-inbox` incoming quote and booking requests (new / booked / done) with suburb, job type, urgency, photo thumbnails, one-tap call and “book a slot”; `#admin-jobs` job list; `#admin-calendar` week of bookable slots |

## Feeling from the scope

Visitor (homeowner with a leak): "Someone local can come today, and I can call in one tap." Calm relief, not panic; nothing to hunt for.
Owner: "I can finally change my own site and see every job that comes in, without losing my Google spot." In control, not dependent on a developer.
Avoid: clutter, walls of repeated text, stock-photo cheesiness, anything that looks like it could drop their rankings.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel they have a "modern, fast, mobile-friendly site" with a "tap-to-call button on every page" and a "shorter, cleaner homepage" "focused on Parramatta and Western Sydney", while "protecting our rankings is very important" and the owner can "easily update ourselves".

**Bookings in, rankings safe.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — relieved
- Feeling word: relieved
- Inner voice: “A plumber for Parramatta is on the other end of one tap.”
- Design levers: hero flush under the nav; a 3-line H1; Space Grotesk wordmark at 64px / 48px; one copper Tap to call action; a real tap-repair photograph, not a house exterior; sticky call bar on every public phone frame.

#### Explore — local
- Feeling word: local
- Inner voice: “Blocked drains, hot water, and the suburb I live in — all sitting in reach.”
- Design levers: three trade-photo service cards; an Areas index of all 60 Western Sydney suburb URLs; the Parramatta suburb page kept at /plumber-parramatta.

#### Decide — sure
- Feeling word: sure
- Inner voice: “The licence and reviews will sit here once they connect them. The path is already short.”
- Design levers: labelled reviews band (“Google reviews appear here — connected at build”); licence strip with “[Licence no. — added by client]”; no invented ratings, prices or years; a three-step path from call to booked slot.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “Job type, suburb, a photo, a preferred time — then send.”
- Design levers: the quote form on `#quote` with those five fields; 52px copper Send button that never clips; the same tap-to-call control still in reach.

#### After — steady
- Feeling word: steady
- Inner voice: “New requests land next to green 301 ticks. The old suburb URLs still point home.”
- Design levers: the owner dashboard standout on `#admin-inbox` (requests beside the redirect map); job list and week calendar; Elementor-style text and photo editing plus a how-to-edit video tile; SEO titles, Search Console connected, and the handover checklist in the client’s name.

Never feel: lost in a 9,000-word homepage, hunting for a phone number, as if the rankings were left to chance, locked out of their own pages, or staring at an empty photo box.

## Standout moment

The owner’s inbox: new quote requests arrive with suburb, job type, urgency and photos, and one tap to call or book a slot — sitting beside a redirect map that shows all 60 suburb pages pointed at their new addresses, each with a green 301 tick. That is “bookings in, rankings safe” on one screen (`#admin-inbox`).

## Key design ideas

Drawn from the client’s own brief:

1. **The owner’s desk is the point.** Most screens are the WordPress-style dashboard: incoming requests, the job list, bookable calendar slots, an Elementor-style editor with a how-to-edit video, the 301 map, SEO, Search Console and handover in their name.
2. **Rankings kept, URLs kept.** /plumber-parramatta stays. All 60 suburb pages are listed and 301-mapped, with a coverage meter at 60 of 60.
3. **Phone first, one copper action.** A shorter Parramatta homepage, a sticky tap-to-call bar on every public phone frame, and a short quote form. Space Grotesk only, fitted tight and geometric, with one copper accent for call and quote.

## Rationale

No logo, colours, reviews or licence were supplied, so the layout is built from the post and fitted to Space Grotesk (L-114): modern, edgy, high contrast, no serif. Your Plumbing Co and all copy are labelled sample content. Trade photographs show actual plumbing work. Director publishes; this pack is dry-run only.
