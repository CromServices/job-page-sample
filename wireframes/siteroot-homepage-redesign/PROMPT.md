## The build

The client asked us to "refresh the homepage for SiteRoot, an AI website builder that helps Australian small businesses create and manage their websites." "Our current homepage feels too generic. It needs a stronger first impression, a clearer explanation of what SiteRoot does, and a more compelling reason for visitors to sign up." They "like how Durable and Yola make their products easy to understand at a glance" and "want that level of polish with a visual identity that feels like SiteRoot." The page needs "A standout hero section that shows what someone can build with SiteRoot and gives them a clear next step," "A more visual product story using website examples, product screenshots or a short demo concept to show how SiteRoot works," "A clearer path to signup with stronger headlines, a well-structured page and calls to action that feel natural as visitors scroll," and "A polished mobile experience that makes the product easy to understand and the signup button easy to find." "The primary goal is more signups from homepage visitors." "Our audience includes tradies, brokers, consultants and other small business owners who want a professional website they can manage themselves." Full checklist in COVERAGE.md.

## Key ideas

1. **The finished site is the first thing you see.** The hero is a real SiteRoot website (Biglord Mortgage, a Melbourne broker) in a browser frame, not a paragraph describing the product. On the phone, that site sits at the top of the first view, under a sticky "Build my site free" button.
2. **How it works is a short path, then real examples.** Three steps in their own voice (tell us what you do, get a finished website, change it by asking), then their other live example sites as full-width bands: Monstera, Dough and Drip, Lifeline Health, Weslander Uff, Plan My Trip.
3. **One price, and the next step is always in reach.** The hero, a sticky header button, pricing and the closing band all lead to "Build my site free." Pricing is stated once: 14 days free, then A$15/month + GST (A$16.50).

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel they can already see the website they would have, that SiteRoot is for Australian tradies, brokers and consultants, and that signing up is the obvious next step.

**Headline feeling: You can already see the website you'd have, and signing up feels obvious.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — seen
- Feeling word: seen
- Inner voice: “That's a real business website. Mine could look like that.”
- Design levers: hero flush under the SiteRoot logo, a large browser frame of Biglord Mortgage, a 3-line H1, and "Build my site free" in the header and the hero.

#### Explore — curious
- Feeling word: curious
- Inner voice: “I tell them what I do, a site appears, and I can change it by asking. And here are sites already like that.”
- Design levers: the three-step path, then each example site on its own full-width band, not a card grid.

#### Decide — confident
- Feeling word: confident
- Inner voice: “This is for people like me — a broker, a tradie, a consultant — and the site will be found when someone searches.”
- Design levers: “Most jobs start with a search,” written for plumbers, electricians, brokers, consultants and clinics.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “Fourteen days free, one price, no card to start. I'll try it.”
- Design levers: pricing in one place, their inclusions as a ruled list, a green "Start free" button, and the same CTA still in the header.

#### After — in-control
- Feeling word: in-control
- Inner voice: “I can change this whenever the business does, without a web team.”
- Design levers: the closing band, “Launch in minutes” rewritten as a single sentence, and "Build my site free" one more time.

Never feel: a generic website-builder template; a newly invented brand; serif type; eyebrow labels, slogan headings, card grids or pill badges; grey placeholders; a dead gap under the nav; a hero that only describes the product in words.

## Standout moment

The hero shows a finished SiteRoot website immediately. On desktop, Biglord Mortgage — a Melbourne mortgage broker, one of the client's named audiences — fills the right side, flush under the nav, in a simple browser frame. On the phone, that same site is the first thing on the screen, above the headline, with "Build my site free" already in the sticky header. The product is seen, not described.

## Type and colour

- Font: Space Grotesk only (self-hosted). Body 18px desktop / 17px phone. H1 64px desktop (3 lines) / 40px phone (2 lines). 7-step type scale and 4/8 spacing scale in :root.
- Logo: their live mark (`logo.svg`), 64px desktop / 48px phone, with the typeset "SiteRoot" and "Grow from here." wordmark on desktop. On the phone the mark stands alone so the header CTA is not clipped.
- Palette, from their brand: ink #1A2B22, soft #3A4D43, logo green #04A870 (the mark only; 3.07:1 on cream, so not used for text), button green #0B6B4A (white text 6.53:1), deep #08563C, lime #A6DD5B (logo accent), cream #F7F6F0, mint #E8F3EC, white #FFFFFF, line #D5E0D8. No brown or beige.
