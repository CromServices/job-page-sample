# Bid wireframe: Maven Yacht Deliveries, luxury Wix site (Freelancer 40817154)

Quick bid wireframe, 15 Nov 2026, for Director. Built from the portfolio (only citable Wix row: **general-power-wix**, https://www.generalpower.com.au — a trades rebuild, not a marine site; do not present it as a yacht example). Not a full build. Maven has no logo file in the post; the nav uses a simple wordmark in their colours, labelled "Client brand". Do not publish.

## The build

The client wants "an experienced Wix designer to build a complete professional website for my US-based yacht services company, Maven Yacht Deliveries."

Services, quoted from the post: "yacht deliveries, yacht management, maintenance coordination, private owner training and boating safety education."

"The website will have approximately 8 pages, including:" Home, Yacht Deliveries, Yacht Management, Private Owner Training, Wives Save Lives, Maven Academy, About Us, Contact.

They also ask:

- "Will you provide mobile and desktop versions?"
- "Will you include basic SEO?"
- "Can you work directly inside my existing Wix account?"
- "Will I retain full ownership and administrative access?"
- "help with future website updates as the business grows"
- "your best fixed-price quotation and estimated completion time"

They will evaluate "Have you designed luxury marine, yacht or premium service-company websites?" and "Can you show me three examples of your best Wix websites?" Their budget: "My budget is $300–$500 USD for the complete project."

## Feeling from the scope

Quoted from the post: "I want a premium, luxury marine appearance using navy, gold, white and ocean blue." The services are about trust: someone else moving, managing or teaching on a very valuable boat, and "boating safety education" for the people aboard.

## Concept

Quiet luxury on open water. Deep navy and white carry the page; gold is used sparingly as a thin rule and on the main buttons; ocean blue softens the safety sections. The hero is a yacht on calm open water. Each of the eight pages appears as a section of the home flow: services, how a delivery works, owner training, Wives Save Lives, Maven Academy, about, and a contact form that asks for the route and the vessel.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: the "premium, luxury marine appearance" of a "complete professional website", in "navy, gold, white and ocean blue", from a company that handles "yacht deliveries, yacht management, maintenance coordination, private owner training and boating safety education." (Quoted from the client post.)

**Entrusted: my yacht, and my family, are in steady hands.**

Not a task. The calm of handing over the keys and not worrying once.

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — prestige
- Feeling word: prestige
- Inner voice: “Deep blue water, a white hull, a thin line of gold. This is the standard my boat deserves.”
- Design levers: full-bleed photograph of a yacht on open water flush under the navy nav; navy scrim so white type holds 4.5:1; a thin gold rule above a two-to-three-line H1; gold primary button; wide spacing and few words.

#### Explore — trust
- Feeling word: trust
- Inner voice: “Deliveries, management, maintenance, all under one name. They know boats like mine.”
- Design levers: service cards with a gold top rule and real marine photographs; plain, unhurried copy; Academy courses and About in the same calm grid; all eight pages visible as a quiet list.

#### Decide — assurance
- Feeling word: assurance
- Inner voice: “Survey, prepare, passage, handover. And my wife could bring us home if she had to.”
- Design levers: four-step delivery story on navy; owner training told as one-to-one time aboard; Wives Save Lives in soft ocean blue with life jackets on the shore, framed as care, not fear.

#### Act — ease
- Feeling word: ease
- Inner voice: “From, to, length, dates. That is all they need from me.”
- Design levers: a contact form that asks about the route and the vessel; service select covering all six offers; full-width gold button; 50px fields; no clutter.

#### After — calm
- Feeling word: calm
- Inner voice: “Sent. Nothing more to do but wait for the plan.”
- Design levers: an ocean-blue confirmation with one sentence of reassurance; no upsell; footer repeating the services in full.

Never feel:

- That the boat is just another job
- Flashy or showy, like a charter ad
- Frightened by safety messaging
- Unsure what happens during a delivery
- Pushed into a call before they are ready

## Rationale

The client wants luxury on a small budget, and they asked for three marine examples we do not have. The honest answer is a concept made for Maven itself: their colours, their eight pages, their services named exactly as they wrote them. The design stays restrained so it can be built quickly in Wix with standard sections. Course names and About copy are labelled "Sample content".
