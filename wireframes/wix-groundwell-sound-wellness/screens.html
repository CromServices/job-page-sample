Sound

Sound baths, collaborative evenings and individual sound sessions, with singing bowls, gong and other instruments.

See upcoming datesQuestions before booking?

About sound

In a sound bath you rest, lying or seated, while instruments are played around you. There is nothing to do and nothing to get right. Many people simply notice the room grow quieter, and themselves with it.

What to expect

Arrive ten minutes early to settle in. Mats, cushions and blankets are provided. Sessions begin and end gently, and you are welcome to stay a few minutes afterwards.

About Reiki

Reiki is a gentle, hands-on or hands-near practice. You rest, fully clothed, while the practitioner works slowly and quietly. It can be received in an individual session, at a community clinic, or learned through certification.

What to expect

Sessions start with a short conversation about how you are and anything you would like the practitioner to know. You can ask for changes at any point.

Tuning Forks

Explore sound and vibration through tuning forks, in a workshop or an individual session.

See upcoming datesQuestions before booking?

About tuning forks

Tuning forks give a clear, pure tone. In a session they are struck and held near the body, or a weighted fork is rested gently so you feel the vibration as well as hear it.

What to expect

Sessions are quiet and unhurried. You stay clothed and comfortable, seated or lying down, and can say if you would prefer the forks held further away.

About somatics

Somatic practices bring attention back to the body through small, slow movements, breath and touch. Nothing is strenuous, and every practice can be done seated.

What to expect

Wear comfortable clothes. Mats, chairs and props are provided. Each workshop ends with a short practice to take home.

Sound & Strings

Live violin joins singing bowls and gong for a special collaborative sound experience.

With Avery and a guest violinist

Who this is for

Anyone who would like to rest and listen. No experience of sound baths or music is needed.

What we’ll do

Settle onto a mat or chair. Avery plays bowls and gong while the violin weaves through, quiet at first, then fuller, then quiet again.

What is included

Mats, bolsters, blankets and eye pillows. Herbal tea afterwards.

What to wear & bring

Warm, comfortable layers and socks.
