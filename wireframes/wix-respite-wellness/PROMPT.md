# Bid wireframe: L.E.X.I.S. Respite & Wellness Inc., existing Wix site redesign

Quick bid wireframe, 16 Nov 2026, for Director. Built from the portfolio (only citable Wix row: **general-power-wix**, https://www.generalpower.com.au — a trades rebuild, not a care-site example; do not present it as one, and do not invent graphic-design samples). Existing site: https://lexisrespitewellness.ca. Real logo, colours and public wording are used. Photographs on the page are free-licence care images used as layout stand-ins, not claimed as their staff. Not a full build. Do not publish.

## The build

"L.E.X.I.S. Respite & Wellness Inc. is looking for an experienced Wix designer who can also support branded visual content."

"Our Wix website is already built, and our logo, brand colours, written content, service information, and most images are already prepared. We are not looking for a full website build from scratch. We need someone to professionally redesign and polish the existing site so it feels modern, warm, trustworthy, accessible, and professional."

Scope of work, quoted from the post:

- "Redesign and polish 6 existing pages: Home, About, Services, Areas We Serve, Careers, and Contact"
- "Optimize the site for mobile and tablet"
- "Improve layout, spacing, typography, navigation, and calls-to-action"
- "Integrate Jotform for intake, inquiries, consultations, and applications"
- "Complete basic SEO, including page titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and local SEO"
- "Improve accessibility and readability"
- "Optimize images for faster loading"
- "Create a recognition section featuring our Futurpreneur/RBC Rock My Business Award and Bramptonist recognition"
- "Work within our existing branding and imagery"
- "Include at least 2 revision rounds"

They would "also prefer someone who can support future branded content such as flyers, social media graphics, Canva templates, and website visuals."

"Preferred completion: within 2–4 weeks of project start." "Budget: approximately $600–$800 CAD, with some flexibility for the right fit."

"Canadian-based freelancers are preferred, especially those familiar with Ontario businesses, accessibility, and local SEO." Website: lexisrespitewellness.ca

## Feeling from the scope

The site should feel "modern, warm, trustworthy, accessible, and professional." Visitors — often family members — should feel "warmly received, safe, and able to trust the people behind the service." Arriving, "welcomed and at ease," not "cold or clinical." They should be able to "read about services and areas served without strain, including on a phone," and reach out "for intake, an inquiry, a consultation, or a career application without feeling lost or talked down to."

## Concept

Their own logo, brown, cream, peach and soft blue, kept and given more air. The hero is a warm visit, not a clinic. Six pages sit in one flow: services in their own words, the GTA areas they already name, a quiet recognition of the two awards they asked to feature, a kind careers path, and four clear doors into Jotform — intake, enquiry, consultation, application.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: a site that is "modern, warm, trustworthy, accessible, and professional," where families are "warmly received, safe, and able to trust the people behind the service," "welcomed and at ease," not "cold or clinical," and able to reach out "without feeling lost or talked down to."

**Warmly received, and safe to trust these people.**

Not a task. The relief of finding a kind, professional door.

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — welcome
- Feeling word: welcome
- Inner voice: “A warm room, a familiar kindness. We are in the right place.”
- Design levers: their handshake-and-heart mark at a readable size on a white plate; cream and brown from their brand; a photograph of a calm visit flush under the nav; a two-to-three-line H1; their own line, “Compassionate care. Meaningful support. Stronger communities.”

#### Explore — understood
- Feeling word: understood
- Inner voice: “Services and neighbourhoods, written so a tired family can read them.”
- Design levers: their four service pillars in open cards; About in their public wording; the GTA area list as plain, large chips; 18px body type and easy contrast.

#### Decide — confident
- Feeling word: confident
- Inner voice: “They are proud of the work, and there is a place here if I wanted to join.”
- Design levers: a recognition section for the Futurpreneur / RBC Rock My Business Award and Bramptonist recognition, proud rather than loud; a careers path with the same calm voice.

#### Act — ease
- Feeling word: ease
- Inner voice: “The form is gentle. No one is talking down to us.”
- Design levers: four clear doors — intake, enquiry, consultation, application; their phone, email and hours; 50px fields; a full-width brown button that does not clip.

#### After — dependable
- Feeling word: dependable
- Inner voice: “Recognised here, and still easy to reach.”
- Design levers: a kind confirmation; their logo again in the footer; no upsell.

Never feel:

- Hospital-sterile, or pitied
- Childish, or like a luxury spa
- Cluttered or hard to read
- Talked down to
- Lost on the way to getting in touch
