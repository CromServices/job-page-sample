Who we are

L.E.X.I.S. Respite & Wellness Inc.

We provide compassionate respite, personal support, and community-based services for children, youth, adults, seniors, and individuals with developmental disabilities across the Greater Toronto Area.

Our mission is to promote independence, inclusion, dignity, and well-being through personalized, holistic care that empowers individuals and strengthens families.
