## The build

The client asked for "a simple, modern, fresh and professional website" whose "main priority is attracting wholesale enquiries and making it easy for businesses to contact us". The homepage top needs the "Cookie Dough Kitchen logo", a "Large, beautiful cookie photograph", "Life’s better with cookies.", a short introduction and a "Prominent “Wholesale Enquiries” button". Then "our five signature flavours, each with a photograph and a short description", Wholesale Information as "the most important section of the website", Our Products, About Us, and a contact form with business name, contact name, email, phone, business location, type of business and message. "I do not require online ordering, customer accounts or payment facilities at this stage." Full checklist in COVERAGE.md.

## Key ideas (from the client's own brief)

1. **The freezer does the hard part.** Their own line, "No mixing. No mess. Just delicious freshly baked cookies.", is the page's one big beat, backed by the real carton facts (48 portions, 8 packs of 6, 185°C, 12–14 minutes).
2. **Five flavours, five pack colours.** Each flavour is a full-width band in its own pouch colour (cream, mint, pink, light blue, soft lemon) with the real pouch photo, the way the packs sit on a shelf. No card grid.
3. **Wholesale is the obvious next step.** "Wholesale Enquiries" in the header and hero, "Become a Wholesale Customer" under the selling points, and a seven-field form that ends in "Request Wholesale Information".

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel that these are premium, fun New York-style cookies, and that stocking them is easy and low-risk for a busy café. The client's own words: "Fun, fresh and welcoming", "Colourful without being overwhelming", "Professional enough to attract wholesale customers", "a boutique Australian cookie brand with a little cheekiness".

**Headline feeling: Fresh New York-style cookies on my counter tomorrow, with no mixing and no mess.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — hungry
- Feeling word: hungry
- Inner voice: “These look properly good, and I can bake them straight from frozen.”
- Design levers: hero flush under the nav on their mint, real palm-tree logo, a close-up of a broken-open cookie, a 3-line H1, the tagline, their intro, and the Wholesale Enquiries button.

#### Explore — delighted
- Feeling word: delighted
- Inner voice: “Each flavour has its own colour and personality.”
- Design levers: five pouch-colour bands with real pack photos and their one-line descriptions; Island Bliss in soft lemon.

#### Decide — confident
- Feeling word: confident
- Inner voice: “Less labour, less waste, consistent portions. That works for my kitchen.”
- Design levers: the eight selling points as a ruled list beside the real carton label; no price list in the way.

#### Act — easy
- Feeling word: easy
- Inner voice: “One short form and I’m done.”
- Design levers: the seven fields they listed, a type-of-business choice, one green button.

#### After — reassured
- Feeling word: reassured
- Inner voice: “A real local business on Magnetic Island, and my details are handled properly.”
- Design levers: About in their words, “Thank you for supporting local”, Facebook and Instagram links, a Privacy view, the tagline signing off the footer.

Never feel: cluttered or overwhelming; childish fun that undercuts wholesale professionalism; rustic, brown or earthy bakery cliché (no beige, no wood boards); stock-cookie or placeholder photography; unsure how to make a wholesale enquiry; a shop, cart, login or public wholesale price list; slogan stacks, eyebrow labels or empty marketing language.

## Standout moment

Straight after the hero, "No mixing." and "No mess." are struck through by a hot-pink line that draws itself, then "Just delicious freshly baked cookies." lands in carton green beside frozen portions on a tray. Under it, the carton facts in one sentence. Reduced-motion users get the strike already drawn.
