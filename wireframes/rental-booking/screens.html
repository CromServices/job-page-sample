Dune House

Saltwood Bay, WA (sample place) · Sleeps 4 · 2 bedrooms · 1 bathroom
Sample stay

A quiet timber house on the dunes

Dune House sits in coastal heath above the bay, a short sandy path from the water. Mornings start on the deck as the light comes up pink over the sea. Inside it's linen, whitewashed boards and a wide window that frames the dunes from the sofa.

Two bedrooms, one bathroom, a full kitchen and a wood heater for winter. Best for a couple or a small family who want the sound of the sea and not much else.
