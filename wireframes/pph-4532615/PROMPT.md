# Prompt — Cloaked Table Events (concept)

A Home wireframe plus an Upcoming Events programme for Cloaked Table Events, a new UK immersive entertainment brand. The only concept markers are the page title and one quiet line in the first viewport. Dates on the programme are illustrative nights at Rise Hall, the venue named on the artwork they already attached.

## Coverage checklist

| Requirement | Screen / section |
|-------------|------------------|
| Homepage: dramatic introduction, featured event, clear booking | `#home` |
| Upcoming Events: searchable listings with dates, locations, prices and booking links; date and location filters | `#events` |
| Our Experiences: overview of event formats | `#experiences` |
| The Cloaked Table: dedicated flagship experience page | `#table` |
| The Cloaked Trials: dedicated full-day experience page | `#trials` |
| Corporate & Private Events: information and booking enquiry form | `#corporate` |
| Venues & Partnerships: information for prospective venue partners | `#venues` |
| About / Contact: company information and enquiry form | `#contact` |
| Dark, cinematic, atmospheric design with dramatic photography and gold and red accents | `#home` hero, using their attached Rise Hall poster |
| Mobile-friendly, user-friendly layout | responsive CSS on every screen |
| Social links, ticket booking path, enquiry forms | footer; event rows; `#corporate` and `#contact` forms |

## The build

The client asked for a site that "feels like entering a world of mystery and suspense", "dark, cinematic and atmospheric, incorporating dramatic photography, video, elegant typography and subtle gold and red accents". Events are "based around 'The Traitors' TV programme". They want "approximately 6–8 pages or templates" and "are happy to combine pages where appropriate to remain within budget". They "like the event listings and search functionality of www.murder57.com, but our website must have its own distinctive design and brand identity".

They already attached a poster for The Cloaked Table at Rise Hall: a lit candle, gold lettering, and the lines "A live immersive experience" and "Secrets. Strategy. Suspicion." This concept uses that artwork, keeps the site on a path their team could later manage in Wix, and shows Home plus the events programme first.

## Lead idea

The first screen should feel like crossing the threshold of a hall that has already gone quiet, then the next night at Rise Hall should be easy to find and book.

## Design reference

Their own attached poster (candle, gold, black, Rise Hall). Listings and date/location search are inspired by the *function* of murder57.com, not its look.

## Distinctive layout move

A typographic evening programme: date stacked in gold at the left of a full-width rule, not a grid of event cards.

## Real copy

Brand and experience names, Rise Hall, and poster lines come from the job post and the attached artwork. Programme dates are illustrative concept nights at that same hall, not claimed past events.

## Type & spacing scale

Space Grotesk only (L-114). Body 18px / 1.5. Steps: 15, 16, 18, 24, 32, 40, 56, 88. Space: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Gutter clamp(20px, 4vw, 64px).

## Client design direction

Build out their poster, not a new identity. The live site can use their supplied serif wordmark as an image; UI type on this concept stays Space Grotesk.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel they have entered a world of mystery and suspense: quiet enough to lean in, sure which night is theirs, and ready to book or enquire without the page turning into a generic ticket shop.

**Headline feeling: The hall has gone quiet, and the next night is obvious.**

**Register: cinematic suspense.** Dark, gold and a restrained red, unhurried. Not a bright brochure, not a marketplace.

**Sell / hook**
- Desire: someone who wants an immersive evening of mystery, deception and theatrical dining, or a private corporate night of the same kind.
- Hook: their candle-lit Rise Hall poster, then one featured night with a clear booking path.
- Objection killers: “Will this still be us?” (their poster, their three experience names, Rise Hall); “Will it feel like Murder 57?” (a typographic programme, not a copied card layout); “Can we add dates later?” (the list is a single collection that can be filtered, added to, or opened).
- The one conversion action: book a public night, or write about a private table.

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — hush
- Feeling word: hush
- Inner voice: “The hall has already gone quiet.”
- Design levers: the full-viewport candle, gold wordmark, a short prose opening, no card grid in the first screen.

#### Enter — invited
- Feeling word: invited
- Inner voice: “This is an evening I would dress for.”
- Design levers: one featured night at Rise Hall, typographic not boxed, a single booking line.

#### Choose — certain
- Feeling word: certain
- Inner voice: “Rise Hall in February is the night.”
- Design levers: the programme list with date and location filters, gold dates on a full-width rule, booking on each row.

#### Ask — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “I’d write to them about a private table.”
- Design levers: the corporate and contact enquiry forms in the same dark register, social links close at hand.

Never feel: a bright brochure, a card grid of events, a generic ticket marketplace, or a copied Murder 57 layout.

## Standout moment

The typographic programme after the candle: dates in gold on a rule, filters for date and location, and their Rise Hall artwork holding the first viewport.

## Feeling from the scope

Suspense that still leaves a clear next step: book a night, or write about a private one.
