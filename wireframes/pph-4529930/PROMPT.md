# Bid wireframe: MIR UK Framer homepage restyle (PeoplePerHour 4529930)

Quick pre-bid wireframe, 18 Nov 2026, for Director. The existing Framer site has no public URL in the post. No separate brief file was attached. The homepage restyle is built around the client’s metallic MIR UK logo and the two supplied photographs. Any wording on the page is labelled Sample content. Dry-run publish only.

## The build

Quoted from the client post:

- "We’re a new UK metal recovery and dismantling contractor."
- "Our website is already built in Framer, with all pages, copy and SEO done."
- "restyle the homepage so it looks and feels built around our logo"
- "a modern, clean, industrial look, with more silver and steel detailing to match our metallic logo"
- "Cleaner and more modern."
- "I can give some inspirations sites."
- "Restyle the homepage in our existing Framer project (desktop and phone)"
- "Set up the colour, text and button styles so we can apply the look to the other pages"
- "Use our own logo and photos (provided)"
- "Please don’t: change copy, page addresses or SEO settings, or publish the site."

## Feeling from the scope

Quoted from the post: "restyle the homepage so it looks and feels built around our logo"; "a modern, clean, industrial look, with more silver and steel detailing to match our metallic logo"; "Cleaner and more modern." A visitor arrives and the metallic mark is already setting the materials of the page — navy from the disc, silver and steel from the letters, the gasholder and refinery photographs held in a clean industrial frame. The owner should feel the homepage is their brand rather than a generic template, and that the same styles will carry to the other existing pages.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: "a modern, clean, industrial look, with more silver and steel detailing to match our metallic logo", "Cleaner and more modern", and that the homepage "looks and feels built around our logo".

**The homepage already feels built from the same steel as the mark.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — grounded
- Feeling word: grounded
- Inner voice: “The mark sits in steel. The plant is already on the page.”
- Design levers: hero flush under the nav; the metallic logo at a readable height on a steel plate; a 2-line H1 in Space Grotesk; one short labelled sample lede; a single unclipped Get in touch button; the gasholder photograph filling the right column, with the mark visible on the worker’s jacket.

#### Explore — steady
- Feeling word: steady
- Inner voice: “The photographs sit in a clean industrial frame. Nothing decorative gets in the way.”
- Design levers: the refinery-dismantling photograph in a machined silver frame; short captions that only name the supplied pictures; calm spacing; no invented project stories, clients or results.

#### Decide — sure
- Feeling word: sure
- Inner voice: “The same silver and steel will hold the rest of the site.”
- Design levers: a quiet band stating that colour, type and buttons are set from the logo; the style-setup frame with reusable tokens; no new pages and no change to existing addresses.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “A clear path to get in touch. Nothing stands clipped or crowded.”
- Design levers: a sample Get in touch control in the header and at the close of the homepage; 52px buttons that never clip; no invented addresses, phone numbers or prices; existing contact details left as they are.

#### After — carried
- Feeling word: carried
- Inner voice: “The colours, type and buttons are already set for the other pages.”
- Design levers: style-setup frame with logo-sampled swatches, a Space Grotesk type scale, primary and ghost button states, the logo plate and the steel hairline; other existing pages left untouched in this pass.

Never feel: generic or decorative, unsure that the logo set the materials, lost behind invented claims, stuck in front of an empty picture box, or as if the other pages will need a different look.

## Standout moment

A light sheen moves slowly across the logo plate, the primary button, and the photograph frame — the same kind of light that runs over the brushed steel of the mark. It is a CSS-only highlight, quiet and performant. When the viewer prefers reduced motion, the sheen holds still as a static glint rather than travelling.

## Key design ideas

Drawn from the client’s own brief:

1. **Built around the metallic logo.** Navy, silver and steel are sampled from the mark. The logo sits at a readable height on a machined plate, and the gasholder photograph shows that same mark already on the work.
2. **Clean, modern, industrial.** Tight Space Grotesk, generous spacing, steel hairlines and photograph frames. No decorative clutter, no serif, no stock photography.
3. **Styles that carry.** Colour, text and button styles are documented on a style-setup frame so the same look can be applied to the other existing pages, which are not redesigned here.

## Rationale

No public URL, Figma file or written brief was supplied, and inspiration sites were mentioned but not given, so the restyle is fitted to the logo, the two photographs, and the written industrial direction. All wording is labelled sample content and kept minimal, because the existing homepage copy was not provided and must not be rewritten or invented as fact. The later build stays in their existing Framer project, with copy, addresses and SEO left as they are. Framer examples belong in the bid letter, not on the page.
