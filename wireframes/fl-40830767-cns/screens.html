Our technology

S-Gate Valve

A gate valve that can be installed without disrupting supply. Designed and tested in accordance with Australian Standards, in high-grade ductile iron.

DN80 – DN600. Gen II appraised by Water Services Association Australia to PN16.

Hydra-Cut

Installs a hydrant under pressure using hot tapping technology. Live hydrants can be installed without interrupting the water supply to customers.

DN100mm – DN600mm. PN16 rated. Suitable on both PE and metallic pipes.

Seven things on the public pages

Checked on civilnsupplies.com on 23 Nov 2026. Nothing was changed.

The Privacy Policy link goes nowhere.In the footer of every page it points back to the same page, and there is no privacy page at the usual address.
The homepage does not say what CNS makes.It opens with “Welcome To CNS Engineering Australia” over a city photo. S-Gate Valve is only in the menu, and Hydra-Cut sits third, below Manufacturing and Utility construction.
The documents are hard to tell apart.Four PDFs sit on two pages, and one link reads “Click for CNS Engineering Australia”. The files are 0.2, 5.0, 5.0 and 9.5 MB.
The media library holds extra copies.The same three S-Gate Valve files appear twice, and one file is called “Untitled document”.
Some pages are live but easy to miss.Novus Gripper is published but not in the menu. Innovations lists three products as “currently trialling”.
One standard is named two ways.The homepage says AS 1481 – 2013 for repair and offtake clamps. The S-Gate Valve and Services pages say AS4181 – 2013.
The enquiry form is the same on every page.We have not sent a test message. We try it end to end once we have access.

Five fixes, in this order

A full backup comes before the first change. You get the list before anything is hidden.

The privacy link.It is on every page, and a customer can see it does nothing.
The homepage line and the products.It is the first thing a buyer reads, and the words already exist on your S-Gate Valve and Hydra-Cut pages.
