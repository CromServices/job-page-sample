About the studio

Central Queensland, Australia

Who we are

Embody Building Design is a boutique residential building design studio based in Central Queensland.

We specialise in custom new homes and renovations. We aim for homes that are thoughtful, functional and pleasing to live in, and that reflect the needs and lifestyles of the people who live there.

The work starts with the block, the climate and how you want to live, and it ends with clear drawings your builder can build from.

New homes, renovations and design

New homes

A custom home starts with the block, the climate and the way you live. We design the plan, the rooms and the way the house meets its land, then prepare the drawings your builder and certifier work from.

Renovations

Renovations and extensions that respect the house you have. We look at what to keep, what to open up and where new rooms should sit, so the old and the new read as one home.

Residential design

Residential design for homes of every size. Layout, light, ventilation and outlook are worked out together, on paper, before anything is built.

Building design

Building design is the licensed practice behind the drawings: clear, buildable documents that carry the design through to the builder.

The house

A new home on a ridge block, set low among the gums.

The bedrooms sit quietly at the back. Living and kitchen run through the middle of the plan and open to a deep verandah and the bush beyond.

Timber ceilings carry the eye outside, the roof reaches well over the verandah, and every room looks onto the garden or the trees.

Plan

Ground floor. Bedrooms at the back, living and kitchen in the middle, entry and verandah at the front.

Mango Tree House

The Range, Rockhampton

Renovation and extension

The house

A high-set timber house with good bones and a tired back end.

We kept the front rooms, the verandah and the boards underfoot. A new family room and deck sit to the side, looking to the garden and the mango tree.

The old house stays the old house. The new rooms are quiet beside it, in darker timber, and they open the home to the yard.

Plan

Existing house in grey, new rooms in clay. The family room opens to a new deck.
