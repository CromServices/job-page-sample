## Feeling from the scope

Quoted from the post: "refined, contemporary, timeless and visually impressive"; "easy to navigate and functional"; "projects and imagery to be the main focus"; "clean layouts, considered typography and a premium design aesthetic"; "understated sophistication over excessive animation"; "thoughtful, functional and aesthetically pleasing homes"; "reflects the quality of our work and attracts prospective clients."

The journey should feel like walking through a considered Central Queensland house: quiet, confident, unhurried. A prospective client arrives, sees the work first, understands that this is a boutique residential building design studio, and can enquire without noise or decoration getting in the way.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: "refined, contemporary, timeless and visually impressive", that the site is "easy to navigate and functional", and that "projects and imagery" are "the main focus", with "understated sophistication over excessive animation".

**The homes come first, and the visitor already feels they have walked through one.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — unhurried
- Feeling word: unhurried
- Inner voice: “Someone has taken care over this. I am not being rushed.”
- Design levers: a full-width still of a Central Queensland house directly under the nav; the studio name and one enquiry action in the upper hero; a 2 to 3 line headline in Space Grotesk; a wordmark at 64px tall that the client's logo replaces; no slider and no motion.

#### Explore — absorbed
- Feeling word: absorbed
- Inner voice: “I want to see the next one. I can almost hear that verandah.”
- Design levers: the Portfolio as a vertical sequence of large stills with the name and place in the margin; no thumbnail grid; each home opens as one continuous essay with stills, rooms and a plan in a single column.

#### Decide — assured
- Feeling word: assured
- Inner voice: “They know this climate and these blocks. This is the kind of home I would want.”
- Design levers: a plain studio statement with the client's own words; four services written as short prose; a simple plan drawn clean beside the photographs; a short client note on each project page; the licensed practice line in the footer.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “I know what to say to them, and it will not take long.”
- Design levers: an Enquire button in the header on every screen; a short enquiry form at the end of each project, so the visitor does not have to leave the story; a full Contact form with the project type; 52px buttons that wrap rather than clip.

#### After — supported
- Feeling word: supported
- Inner voice: “They will answer, and the studio can keep the site fresh without calling a developer.”
- Design levers: a calm confirmation after sending; the Wix Studio hand-off screen where a new home is added by filling in name, place, stills and short text and pressing Publish; one plan covering hosting, forms and basic SEO.

Never feel: rushed past the photographs, lost in a menu, sold to with badges and slogans, shown thumbnails instead of homes, unsure how to enquire, or as if the studio's own team cannot update the site.

## Standout moment

The visitor opens one home and stays in that story. The hero still, a short essay, a simple plan, the rooms and a short client note unfold as one continuous page, and the enquiry sits at the end of the story rather than only on a separate Contact page. On the Wix Studio hand-off screen the studio can see that every project page is made the same way, from one Projects collection, so adding the next home is filling in the name, place, stills and short text.

## Key design ideas

1. **The homes come first.** Large stills lead every screen. Names and places sit in the margin, so the photograph is the first read and the words stay short. There is no card grid anywhere on the site.
2. **One essay for each home.** A project page is a single column: hero still, short text, plan, rooms, a client note and the enquiry. The visitor never has to leave the story to get in touch.
3. **Quiet by design.** Deep charcoal, warm off-white and one earth accent, in Space Grotesk only. The only motion is a colour change on hover. The logo, colours and photography swap in when Embody sends them.

## Rationale

Embody has a logo, a colour palette and a Brand Brief, none of which came with the post, so this wireframe does not invent a competing identity. The palette is restrained and the logo slot is obvious. Wix Studio is the later build because the studio can add and edit project pages itself from a CMS template, with hosting, forms and basic SEO on one plan: about A$18 a month billed yearly, GST included (approximate; confirm at purchase), with the domain renewing separately. Framer is the alternative if the studio later prefers a more art-directed host at a similar monthly cost; it is not built here. Copy uses only building design, residential design, custom home design and building designer.
