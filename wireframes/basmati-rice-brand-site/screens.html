To cook
Rinse until the water runs clear, soak 30 minutes, then simmer covered in 1½ cups of water per cup of rice for 12–15 minutes. Rest 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.

Keep sealed in a cool, dry place.

How to layer a biryani so every grain stays separate

Good biryani starts with the rice. Rinse the basmati until the water runs clear, then soak it for 30 minutes so the grains lengthen without breaking.

Par-boil, then layer

Boil the rice in well-salted water until it is about two-thirds cooked, drain it, and layer it over the marinated chicken. Finish with saffron milk, fried onions and fresh mint, then seal and cook on low heat.
