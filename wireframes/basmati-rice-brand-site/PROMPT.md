## The build

The client asked us to "build a professional website for our basmati rice brand", including "a homepage, product pages, a blog, and a contact page", plus "a simple online store where customers can browse products, add items to a cart, and complete purchases". The site "should be easy to navigate, visually appealing, and optimized for search engines". Full checklist in COVERAGE.md.

## Key ideas

1. **The grain is the hero.** A close, sunlit macro of long, separate basmati grains fills the home page. The product shows itself, not a sack or a label.
2. **Choose by what you cook.** Rice is matched to dishes (biryani and pulao, everyday steamed rice, wholegrain meals) on Home and in the Shop, so the varieties are easy to tell apart.
3. **Two taps to buy.** The product page answers what a cook needs (size, best for, how to cook, storage) right beside Add to cart, then there's a short cart and a store checkout.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel this is an appetising, premium basmati brand they can trust and buy from easily, not a commodity sack listing.

**Headline feeling: The long grain you can almost smell, trusted enough to buy in two taps.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — hungry
- Feeling word: hungry
- Inner voice: “Look at those grains. That's the rice I want in my biryani.”
- Design levers: saffron hero flush under the nav, a 2–3 line H1, a slow drift across the grain macro, Shop and Recipe buttons.

#### Explore — curious
- Feeling word: curious
- Inner voice: “Which one is for pulao, and how do I cook it?”
- Design levers: the Shop as ruled rows matched to dishes, and blog posts with real-looking cooked dishes.

#### Decide — confident
- Feeling word: confident
- Inner voice: “Size, cooking steps, storage, all right here. I know what I'm getting.”
- Design levers: the product page facts list beside the size choice and Add to cart; a blog post that ends with the rice it uses.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “One item, one button, done.”
- Design levers: a one-line cart, then a checkout with contact, delivery and payment, and a single chilli-red Pay now button.

#### After — reassured
- Feeling word: reassured
- Inner voice: “There are people behind this brand I can actually write to.”
- Design levers: Contact with a short form, and recipes and stories that give a reason to come back.

Never feel: a generic grocery template; a cluttered marketplace grid; brown sack-and-burlap rustic cliché; clip-art; serif type; eyebrow labels, slogan headings or pill badges; placeholder image boxes; invented prices, awards or health claims.

## Standout moment

The grain itself. A full-width macro of long, translucent basmati grains, backlit in warm sun, sits under the saffron hero and drifts slowly (a 24-second scale and pan). It stops when prefers-reduced-motion is set. The detail of each grain tip makes the rice the hero, not the packaging.

## Type and colour

- Font: Space Grotesk only (self-hosted). Body 18px desktop / 17px phone. H1 64px desktop (2 lines) / 40px phone (3 lines). 7-step type scale and 4/8 spacing scale in :root.
- Palette: ink #1F1A3A, soft ink #463F63, saffron #FFC83D (hero), pale saffron #FFF4CC, pale coral #FFE1D6, chilli #C8283F (buttons, white text 5.48:1), chilli deep #A61E33, leaf #1E7A4C (price and labels), white #FFFFFF, line #E4DFF0. No brown or earthy beige.
