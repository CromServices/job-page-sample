About this store

This is a concept for a from-scratch Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme, hand-coded in Liquid, HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The post asked for a store that is not built in PageFly, Shogun or GemPages, and that does not lean on a stack of monthly apps.

Luxury D2C brand guidelines were named in the post and were not attached. No business name, current-store URL or logo was supplied, so this page does not invent one. The coat, the prices and the delivery window are merchandising for the concept.

Home, collection, product, about and landing are written as reusable sections a merchant could rearrange in the OS 2.0 editor, with the conversion features coded into the theme.
