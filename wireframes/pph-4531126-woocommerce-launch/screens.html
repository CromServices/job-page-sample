Asset spec

What to photograph, and where it sits in WoodMart.

Advice for the images and video needed to finish a slick, elegant fashion and lifestyle store on the existing WoodMart and WPBakery foundation. Final pixel sizes should be confirmed against the live theme once access is shared, in case a slider height or product ratio is already set.

Asset Size and format Style and placement
Homepage hero, or slider slide 1920 × 800, or 1920 × 1080 if the current slider is closer to 16:9. WebP or high-quality JPEG. Keep each slide under about 300 KB where possible. A calm area on one side for the headline, or type sits on a scrim. No words in the photograph. Soft shop light or daylight. Sits flush under the header, full width, with no empty band.
Optional hero or band video 1920 × 1080, MP4 H.264, 8 to 12 seconds, no sound needed, under 8 MB. A quiet merch or rail loop. Replace the still hero, or sit in a band below it. Provide a still as the fallback.
Product stills 2000 × 2000 (1:1) or 1600 × 2000 (4:5). Pick one ratio and use it for all five products. WebP or high-quality JPEG. Plain wall or soft daylight, true colour, the same crop distance on every piece. Main shot plus two to four gallery angles: front, back, a cloth detail, and a worn view if it can be done. Fills the product card and the product-page gallery.
Hotspot photograph 1920 × 1080 or 1600 × 900, landscape, WebP or JPEG. A rail or a styled look with room for three to five marks, away from busy pattern. Used in WoodMart’s built-in image-hotspot element, on the homepage below the hero.
Optional product video 1080 × 1080 or 1920 × 1080, MP4, 10 to 15 seconds, under 10 MB. A slow turn or a cloth detail. No type on screen. Sits in the product gallery.
Size guide Type and a clean table, not a photograph of a chart. A WoodMart size-guide popup or a product tab, opened from the buy form so it is next to the sizes.
Logo and favicon Logo as SVG, or PNG on a clear ground, plus a dark and a light version if the header changes. Favicon at 32 and 180 pixels. Header wordmark and the browser tab. The live mark replaces the “Client brand” label.

Launch plan

How the store reaches a tested launch, on the foundation already in place.

Staging, backups, payments, shipping, tests and the legal pages — the setup work that sits behind the shop front. WoodMart and WPBakery stay. Anything still unknown is marked to be agreed in the bid.

Staging

Where practical, work is done on a staging copy of the existing site, so the live shop is not the review space. The client reviews the homepage, the five products, checkout and shipping there, and only then is the work moved to the live domain.

Backups

A full backup of the current site and its database is taken before any work starts. Existing content, products and settings are preserved, and a copy is kept until after launch is confirmed.

Payment gateways

WooCommerce checkout is completed with the gateway the client chooses. The preference is unknown — ask in bid. Card, PayPal, or another gateway the client already has in mind can be configured. Any plugin or transaction cost is disclosed before it is added. Checkout runs over a secure connection, with a payment confirmation on success.

UK and international shipping

WooCommerce shipping is set up for the United Kingdom and for international orders, with weight-based rates that add up correctly on multi-item bags. The destination list and the rate table are unknown — ask in bid. Sample figures on the checkout above are for the layout only.

Stock, orders and customer emails

Stock control is switched on for the five products, including variations. Order processing follows WooCommerce’s usual flow: new order, processing, completed. Customer emails (order received, processing, completed, and stock notices) are configured. The wording of those emails is unknown — ask in bid.

End-to-end tests

Before launch, the full purchase path is tested on desktop and on a phone:

  • Add to bag, cart totals, and a variation change
  • Checkout, including a failed and a successful payment
  • UK and international shipping calculations on single-item and multi-item orders
  • Payment confirmation, order emails, and a stock decrement

Legal and content pages

About, Contact, Delivery, Returns, Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions are completed from agreed content and linked in the footer. That copy is unknown — ask in bid. No legal wording is invented for this concept.

SEO, security, privacy and performance

Essential settings are configured before launch: one clear H1 on each template, titles and meta descriptions in the client’s voice, SSL on checkout, privacy and cookie settings to match the Privacy page, and performance basics (image sizes, caching already available on the host). Anything that needs a paid plugin is disclosed in advance.

Stack, costs and timescale

WoodMart and WPBakery are retained. Built-in features cover the hotspots, the filters, the size guide and the product galleries, so extra custom development is avoided unless a gap appears once the site is reviewed. Any plugin, licence or third-party cost is disclosed before it is added. The client asked for a start immediately or within days, and would ideally finish in 2–3 working days, subject to the condition of the site and the assets being available. That window is confirmed in the bid, after a look at the existing site.

The steps

  1. Access, backup, and a short written gap list from the existing site.
  2. Homepage, hotspots, the five products, and the size guide, on staging.
  3. Payments, shipping, emails, legal pages, and essential settings.
  4. End-to-end tests on desktop and phone, then a client review on staging.
  5. Move to the live domain, a final purchase test, and a short hand-off note.
