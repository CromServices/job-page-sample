# Bid wireframe: Wix Studio Two, professional drone technology company

Quick bid wireframe, 16 Nov 2026, for Director. Built from the portfolio (only citable Wix row: **general-power-wix**, https://www.generalpower.com.au — a trades rebuild, not a drone or Wix Studio Two project; do not present it as either). Not a full build. The client has approved Figma, copy and assets, and asked for implementation, not a new design. This pack shows how the live site should feel in their trade, and that the interactive pieces are real components. No company name was given; the nav uses a simple mark labelled "Client brand". Do not publish.

## The build

"We are looking for a developer experienced with Wix Studio to implement a complete website redesign for a professional drone technology company."

"All page designs, copywriting, and visual assets have already been developed and approved. We are looking for implementation, not a new website design."

They provide "Approved Figma designs for approximately 16 pages", "Working HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototypes for each page, including animations, responsive styling, and assets", and "A new, unpublished Wix Studio Two site for development."

"Our Figma designs are desktop-only. The developer is responsible for creating polished, fully responsive tablet and mobile layouts using the original HTML prototypes as references."

Scope, quoted from the post:

- "Implement the approved designs accurately in Wix Studio Two."
- "Build reusable, maintainable components using native Studio Two features and React/TypeScript where appropriate."
- "Preserve and implement animations, interactions, navigation, and functionality."
- "Optimize all pages for desktop, tablet, and mobile."
- "Configure Wix CMS, Blog, Events, Forms, and relevant integrations."
- "Support existing content migration, SEO preservation, and final deployment."
- "Perform thorough QA/testing and provide a clean handoff."

Custom work includes "before/after image comparison sliders, animated visualizations, interactive technical graphics, and other custom UI components", built "natively within Wix Studio Two using Editor React Components (ERCs) where appropriate."

"We do not want static screenshots or embedded HTML substitutes for functional components." "The final website must remain manageable within Wix Studio Two."

## Feeling from the scope

Visitors should feel they have reached "a precise, capable technical firm, not a gadget shop or a generic template." The first screen should feel "exact, calm, and high-craft". Motion should feel "purposeful, not ornamental." The site should "remain trustworthy and easy to live with — still the company's own tool, not a locked black box."

## Concept

A precise instrument, not a gadget shop. Near-black nav, cool grey ground, a single warm metal rule. The hero is a professional camera drone in close detail. A working before-and-after comparison, a technical plate, insights, events and a short enquiry form show that the pieces are real components, still editable, and just as exact on a phone.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: they have reached "a precise, capable technical firm, not a gadget shop or a generic template," with a first screen that is "exact, calm, and high-craft," and a site that remains "the company's own tool, not a locked black box."

**Assured: this work is exact, and in capable hands.**

Not a task. The quiet of a firm that already knows its aircraft.

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — assured
- Feeling word: assured
- Inner voice: “The aircraft, the frame, the quiet. This work is held with care.”
- Design levers: professional camera-drone photograph flush under a near-black nav; a dark scrim so white type holds 4.5:1; a two-to-three-line H1; a thin metal rule; wide space and few words.

#### Explore — curious
- Feeling word: curious
- Inner voice: “The detail is here if I want it, and nothing fights for my attention.”
- Design levers: a real before-and-after slider, not a picture of one; purposeful motion on a technical plate; aerial work and operations photographs; sample captions clearly labelled.

#### Decide — confident
- Feeling word: confident
- Inner voice: “On a phone this would still feel exact. Worth asking further.”
- Design levers: insights and events as native, editable entries; the same typography at every width; a clear path to enquire.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “A clear form, a precise question. That is enough for now.”
- Design levers: a short enquiry form; 50px controls; a studio plate of the aircraft; a full-width primary button that does not clip.

#### After — trust
- Feeling word: trust
- Inner voice: “This stays theirs to run, not a locked box.”
- Design levers: a quiet confirmation; no upsell; a reminder that the site remains a working instrument.

Never feel:

- Cheap or toy-like
- Cluttered, or like generic template noise
- That interactive features are faked
- That the site is a locked black box
- “Tech startup” gimmickry
