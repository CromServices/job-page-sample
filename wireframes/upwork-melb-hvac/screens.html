Questions people ask before a quote

What is the difference between a split system and a ducted system?

A split system treats one room or an open living area, with an indoor unit on the wall and an outdoor unit outside. A ducted system treats the whole house, with air travelling through the ceiling to each room.

How do Victorian Energy Upgrades discounts work?

The program publishes indicative discounts for replacing older heating with an efficient reverse-cycle air conditioner. The work has to be done with an accredited provider, and the exact discount is confirmed on the quote. The checker on this page uses those published figures, and does not invent an amount if your answers do not match a published row.

Do I need to be home for a quote?

A thorough quote usually needs a look at the house, so the system is sized properly. Send the enquiry first and a time can be arranged from there.
