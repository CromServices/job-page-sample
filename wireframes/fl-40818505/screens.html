What we would prioritise first

Three experiments, in order

You asked how we would tackle this and which tools or experiments we would prioritise first. After the outside-in audit of stock-furniture.com, this is the opening sequence — implemented as Shopify theme tweaks, then measured in GA4.

  1. Rio Corner and the other top product pages. Add to Cart above the fold on phone, and the delivered, installed total before the button. Metric: add_to_cart, then purchase.
  2. The cart. Delivery by area and installation as an add-on, so the cost does not reopen at checkout. Metric: begin_checkout, then purchase.
  3. The homepage opening and weight. Fill the empty H1, then reduce the first-load request count. Metric: product views, then purchase.

Heat maps on the top ten product pages, and the full GA4 funnel, start as soon as we have access. Nothing here invents a lift; the test is whether completed transactions move, before and after.
