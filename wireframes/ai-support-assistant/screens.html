Sample article · Delivery

Delivery times by state

Updated by Sam, HelpDesk Hub team2 min read

We pack orders on weekdays. These times start from the day your order is packed, not the day you order.

Western Australia. Perth metro, including Fremantle and Joondalup: 3 to 5 business days. Regional WA: 5 to 9 business days.

Large pots. Pots over 40 cm travel by freight and can take 2 extra days. We'll text you when the freight company has them.

Live plants. We only send plants Monday to Wednesday, so they don't sit in a depot over the weekend.

Sample article · Returns
BIH2ListLink

Plants are living things and sometimes the trip is hard on them. If a plant arrives damaged or unwell, message us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll replace or refund it.

Leaf drop after 14 days. Citrus and other evergreens often drop leaves in their first month while they settle. Send us a photo. If the plant hasn't recovered two weeks later, we'll replace it.

We can't accept returns of plants that have been repotted into the ground.
