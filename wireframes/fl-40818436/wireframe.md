## Build approach (for Director's bid)
- Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme sections built to the supplied designs; product options for size, presentation and frame via native variants plus a product-options app if variants exceed 100 / 3 options.
- Room preview: integrate an existing product-preview app or a small custom section (upload photo, place several artworks, choose frame, save to customer account, share link).
- Fundraising: referral links per school/parish (affiliate app or discount-code attribution), commission report export.
- Supplier sync: supplier's app or feed (to confirm which supplier) for products, stock and order routing.
- Checkout: Shopify Payments, shipping zones, tax settings, order notifications.
- Milestones (suggested): 1 theme + homepage, 2 catalogue + product page, 3 room preview + wedding flow, 4 fundraising + checkout, 5 test on phone/tablet/desktop and handover.
