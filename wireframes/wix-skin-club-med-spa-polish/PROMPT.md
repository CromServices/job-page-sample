# Skin Club Wellness — Wix draft polish preview

A fix-job preview of the client's own live Wix site at https://www.skinclubwellness.com, not a redesign. The public page is a one-page before/after of the four visual tasks in their post, plus the draft-only promise.

## The build

1. "Adjust the card grid layout and text box height so none of the descriptions get cut off"
2. "the cards sit evenly across the page"
3. "Remove the repeated title line inside the paragraph"
4. "expand the text box so the bottom line is not cut off"
5. "Check text margins and button alignment across my 42 CMS treatment pages"
6. "so nothing is cut off or overlapping"
7. "Verify the homepage and dynamic treatment pages look clean on mobile before I publish"
8. "work strictly inside my existing saved draft without publishing until I approve the preview link"

## Scope coverage

| Requirement | Shown in proof as |

## Client design direction

Their own site and brand stay exactly as they are: AF monogram logo, Fraunces headings, light sans body, charcoal header #2C2C2C, forest #243A2A, cream #F1EADA, taupe #BBA283, card cream #EBDECF, and their live copy and photographs. This is a polish of their direction, not a new design.

Key ideas drawn from their site and the brief: calm finish, even spacing, and nothing cut off.

## Feeling from the scope

A med spa owner who is nearly done and frustrated by small flaws: seeing it finished should feel like relief and pride, calm, premium, nothing cut off, ready to publish.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel: the site is “finished”; “calm, premium, nothing cut off”; and “ready to publish.” The owner, reviewing this draft, should feel relief and pride rather than embarrassment at clipped text.

**Finished, and still theirs.**

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — relieved
- Feeling word: relieved
- Inner voice: The loose ends are finally sitting still.
- Levers: a short list of the four fixes; the live site named; “Not published” in the header.

#### Explore — proud
- Feeling word: proud
- Inner voice: This still looks like the practice I meant to show.
- Levers: before/after of their own Signature Services photographs and copy; the slider that opens the cut-off lines.

#### Decide — assured
- Feeling word: assured
- Inner voice: Every treatment page will read clean, the same way.
- Levers: one HydraFacial template, captioned as the layout for all 42 CMS items; margins, overlaps and buttons corrected once.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: When this preview is approved, the draft can go live.
- Levers: the closing draft section, collaborator access, no publish without their say.

#### After — calm
- Feeling word: calm
- Inner voice: Nothing is cut off. The draft can rest.
- Levers: phone frames of homepage and treatment, even cards, a full last line, buttons that sit still.

Never feel: embarrassed by cut-off text; as if the brand was redesigned; rushed into publishing; messy or unfinished.

## Standout moment

A before/after slider on their own Signature Services cards: drag and the “..” truncations resolve into full, evenly sitting cards.
