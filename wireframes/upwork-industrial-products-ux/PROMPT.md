# Prompt — RR Fisher (concept)

A first-stage tidy of the existing R.R. Fisher WordPress catalogue (rrfisher.com.au). Keep their brand, logo, categories and published product data. Do not rebuild the site or invent a new identity. The only concept marker is the page title.

## Coverage checklist

| Requirement | Screen / section |
|-------------|------------------|
| Home, a simple first impression with a clear way into the product categories | `#home` |
| Product-finding path through categories, short and calm | `#find` |
| One existing product page, technical information kept, quote or contact without losing place | `#product`, `#enquire` |
| Existing enquiry path, not a new form system | `#enquire`, `#contact` |

Full checklist in COVERAGE.md.

## The build

The client asked us to "tidy up our existing Australian industrial products website" and to make it "simple, clean, and much easier to navigate, especially through our product categories". The goal, in their words, is "to improve usability and user experience without rebuilding the site from scratch", and "Existing product pages, technical information, and data should remain intact."

RR Fisher already supplies power transmission, conveyor and vibratory equipment to manufacturers in New Zealand and Australia, from Palmerston North, Auckland, Christchurch and Sydney. The live site’s problem, as the client named it, is navigation: a crowded mega-menu makes the four product families hard to use. This concept is a careful first pass — clearer categories, a visible trail, technical data left in place, and the existing Enquire now form brought next to the product.

## Key ideas

- Start from the four families the site already has, not from a new marketing story.
- Replace the mega-menu with a short trail and one step at a time.
- Keep the GE Series copy and data in full.
- Enquire without leaving the product.

## Emotion of the scope

Client wants customers to feel they can find the right coupling, clutch or vibrator through clearer categories, still see the technical detail, and enquire without a fight.

**Headline feeling: I can get to the right item, and I can ask from there.**

**Register: quiet competence.** Practical, unhurried, industrial, not a launch.

**Sell / hook** (for the tidy-up, not a marketing page)
- Desire: a buyer who already knows they need a coupling, clutch, vibrator or controller, and needs to reach the right item through the catalogue.
- Hook: the four families — Couplings, Clutches, Vibratory, Controllers — sit in the first viewport, under the company’s own line, with no mega-menu in the way.
- Objection killers: “Will you strip our technical data?” (the GE Series page keeps the published description, materials, hardness, temperature range and hub styles); “Is this a new brand?” (the R.R. Fisher logo, #FF7100, Roboto, and the real category names stay); “Do we need a new enquiry system?” (the same Name, Email, Phone and Message form, on the product so the place is not lost).
- Proof: the live catalogue structure, the GE Series text, and the published Sydney and Palmerston North contacts.
- The one conversion action: Enquire now.

### Feeling journey

#### Arrive — sure
- Feeling word: sure
- Inner voice: “This is RR Fisher. The four families are right here.”
- Design levers: cleaned logo at 64px (48px on a phone), the quiet concept line, their headline, and a jaw-coupling photograph in the first viewport, with the family index following.

#### Explore — oriented
- Feeling word: oriented
- Inner voice: “Couplings, then Cross & Morse, then Shaft Couplings. I know where I am.”
- Design levers: a persistent trail; typographic rows, not a card grid; partner groups taken from the live menu.

#### Decide — steady
- Feeling word: steady
- Inner voice: “This is the GE Series. The temperature range and hub styles are still here.”
- Design levers: the product photograph, the published data as a readable sheet, Enquire now close at hand.

#### Act — ready
- Feeling word: ready
- Inner voice: “I’ll enquire from this product, not from a blank form on another page.”
- Design levers: an Act band at the same type size as the hook, the existing form wording, the product name kept in view.

#### About — informed
- Feeling word: informed
- Inner voice: “They have been at this for over 70 years, from Palmerston North, Auckland, Christchurch and Sydney.”
- Design levers: the published About wording only, no mix with the footer’s year count.

#### After — settled
- Feeling word: settled
- Inner voice: “They have the message. I also have the Sydney and Palmerston North numbers if I need them.”
- Design levers: a short confirmation in their wording, then the published addresses and telephone numbers.

Never feel: a new brand, a mega-menu, a card grid of categories, earth colours, a stripped-back product page, or a fresh enquiry funnel.

## Standout moment

The family trail: Products / Couplings / Shaft Couplings / GE Series, always visible, so a customer is never lost in the old menu.

The Act moment matches the hook: Enquire now is set in the same large type, on the product, with the existing form.

## Type and colour

- Font: Roboto, self-hosted, as on the live site. Sans only. Weights 400, 500, 700.
- Type scale: 15 / 16 / 17 / 18 / 24 / 32 / 40. Body 18px on desktop, 17px on a phone. Hook and Enquire now share the 40px step.
- Spacing: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64.
- Radii: 8px on controls, never a pill.
- Colour:
  - Paper `#FAFAFA` and white `#FFFFFF` (from their kit)
  - Ink `#111111`
  - Muted `#3A3A3A` (logo grey, used for secondary type)
  - Line `#D5D7DA`
  - Brand `#FF7100` (logo orange). Primary buttons are ink on this orange (AA). Do not use the kit’s rust oranges `#DB6F2D` and `#BE610F`.
- Logo: cleaned R.R. Fisher wordmark, 64px high on desktop, 48px on a phone.

## Images

A matched set of studio photographs of a jaw coupling, on a cool white ground: a wide frame in the home first viewport, and a closer frame on the GE Series page. No empty photo boxes. Category paths stay typographic, not a picture grid. The logo is their file, cleaned only. The product-page photograph is lazy-loaded; the home photograph is not, because it sits in the first viewport.

## Working interactions

1. Opening a family (Couplings, Clutches, Vibratory, Controllers) to see the groups the live menu already uses.
2. Opening Cross & Morse to the Shaft Couplings list, then the GE Series product.
3. Enquire now, on the product, with the existing form, without leaving the item.

## Per-screen spec

### Home (`#home`)
- Logo, then Products, About, Contact.
- A quiet plain-text line at the opening of the first viewport: “Website concept for RR Fisher”. Not a pill or badge. The page title is “Concept: RR Fisher”.
- Hook: “We help industry power ahead.” Then a matched studio photograph of a jaw coupling, in the first viewport, from the same set as the GE Series product shot.
- Supporting line as published: power transmission, conveyor and vibratory equipment, New Zealand and Australia.
- The four families as typographic rows (not a card grid), each with the partner or range names already on the site.

### Find (`#find`)
- Trail at the top of the section. One family at a time. Couplings shows R+W, Cross & Morse and Miki Pulley. Cross & Morse shows the eight Shaft Couplings product names from the live category. Other families show only groups and product names published on the site. No invented items.

### Product (`#product`)
- Trail through Products / Couplings / Shaft Couplings / GE Series.
- Title and the “GE Series Elastomeric Couplings” heading as published.
- The jaw-coupling photograph, then the published description and a compact data list taken only from that text.
- Enquire now, ink on `#FF7100`, 56px high, leading to the Act section without dropping the product name.

### Enquire (`#enquire`)
- Same 40px type as the hook. “Enquire now.” Then: “Please fill in the form and we will get back to you shortly regarding your enquiry.”
- Visible labels: Name, Email, Phone, Message. The product name stays in view when the customer arrived from GE Series. No invented fields. No placeholder attributes.

### About and contact (`#about`, `#contact`)
- About: the published Palmerston North, Auckland, Christchurch and Sydney line, and the work in bulk handling, food processing, mining and manufacturing automation. Use the About wording; do not mix it with the footer’s “over 60 years” line on the same block as “over 70 years”.
- Contact: Sydney (Level 4 / 29 Kiora Road, Miranda, NSW 2228, +61 2 9540 4533) and Palmerston North (680 Tremain Ave, Palmerston North 4414, 0800 235 877). The contact form also has Company name, as on the live contact form.

## Skeleton

Family trail: a single scrolling column with a persistent crumb trail and typographic rows that drill down one step at a time. Not a two-column layout, not a mega-menu, not a card grid.
